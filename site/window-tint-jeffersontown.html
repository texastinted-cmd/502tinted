Short answer: 502 Tinted serves Jeffersontown from its shop in Valley Station, about 30 minutes west on the Gene Snyder Freeway. We install HITEK ceramic window tint from $249 for a car and detail cars, trucks and SUVs.

Serving Jeffersontown (J-town)

Jeffersontown, J-town to most people, is the east-end city along Taylorsville Road and Hurstbourne Parkway, with business parks around Blankenbaker Parkway and the Gaslight Festival every September.

Company cars and commuters parked in business-park lots all day get the full sun through the glass. Ceramic tint keeps the cabin and the dash from cooking.

What we usually recommend here

Ceramic IR on commuter cars; Ceramic Plus for sales and service vehicles that live outside.

Questions we get a lot

How far is 502 Tinted from Jeffersontown?

About 30 minutes on the Gene Snyder Freeway.

Do you tint company vehicles?

Yes. Call or text with the vehicles and we’ll price them.

Is 20% tint legal on my front windows?

No. Kentucky’s front-door limit is 35%. 20% is legal behind the driver.
