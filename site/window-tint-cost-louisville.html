What changes the price

  • How much glass. A coupe has four side windows and a back glass. A three-row SUV can have ten pieces.
  • Which film. Ceramic Plus blocks more heat than Ceramic IR and costs more.
  • Old film. Steaming off old tint and cleaning the glue is extra work, especially on the back glass.
  • Tricky glass. Very curved back glass, split rear windows and small vent windows take more time.
  • The windshield. A sun strip is quick. Full windshield heat film takes longer.

Why the cheapest tint costs more

A low-price tint job is almost always basic dyed film with no ceramic in it. It looks fine at first, then fades purple and bubbles within a few Kentucky summers. Then you pay for removal and for tint again.

Ceramic costs more once and lasts the life of the car. Spread over the years you own it, it’s the cheaper choice.

Questions we get a lot

How much does it cost to tint a car in Louisville?

At 502 Tinted, $249 and up for side windows and back glass with HITEK Ceramic IR, or $349 and up with Ceramic Plus.

How much is it to tint just the front windows?

Front two doors start at $129 in HITEK Ceramic IR.

Why does SUV tint cost more?

More windows. Many SUVs and vans also have small vent windows that take time to fit.

Is ceramic tint worth the extra cost?

Usually. It blocks much more heat than basic film, and it doesn’t need replacing in a few years.

Do you charge to remove old tint?

Yes, removal starts at $99 and depends on the film’s age and how many windows have it.
