Detailing for Kentucky weather

Louisville is hard on cars in every season. Winter brine and road salt sit in the wheel wells and on the lower doors. Spring pollen in the Ohio Valley turns dark paint yellow in a day. Summer brings bugs from every highway drive and sap from parking under trees, and fall leaves stain paint if they sit wet.

A good exterior detail strips all of that off, decontaminates the paint, and adds a sealant so the next round washes off easier. An interior detail gets the salt stains out of the carpet and the crumbs out of the seat tracks.

Twice a year is a good rhythm for most cars: once after winter, once before it.

Pick the right detail

  • Interior detail

    For the inside: carpets, seats, plastics, glass. Pet hair, kids, a commute’s worth of coffee.

  • Exterior detail

    For the paint, wheels and glass: wash, decontamination, clay and sealant.

  • Full detail

    Both, in one visit, from $229. The most popular choice, especially before selling a car.

  • Paint correction

    For swirls, haze and scratches you can see in the sun. One-step or two-step machine polishing.

Questions we get a lot

How much does a car detail cost in Louisville?

At 502 Tinted, a full detail starts at $229 for a car. Interior-only starts at $149, exterior-only at $129, and a maintenance wash at $59.

How long does a full detail take?

Four to six hours for a typical car. Drop it off in the morning and pick it up in the afternoon.

Do you detail SUVs and trucks?

Yes. They have more surface and more interior, so they’re priced after we see them.

How often should I get my car detailed?

A full detail twice a year suits most cars, with maintenance washes in between. Cars that sit outside or carry pets need it more often.
