Why heat rejection matters more than darkness in Kentucky

The heat you feel on your arm through the glass is mostly infrared, not visible light. A dark basic film blocks light but lets plenty of infrared through, which is why a car with cheap limo tint can still be an oven.

Kentucky’s front-door limit is 35%, so you can’t go darker up front to fight the heat. You need a film that blocks infrared at that shade. That’s the whole point of ceramic: a 35% Ceramic Plus front window rejects more solar energy than many dark non-ceramic films.

It works the other way too. If you want a lighter look, a 50% or even 70% ceramic still cuts a noticeable amount of heat.

Which one should you get?

  • Pick Ceramic IR if…

    You want real heat and UV protection at a fair price, your car is a daily commuter, or it’s a lease you’ll turn in. It’s the film most of our customers choose.

  • Pick Ceramic Plus if…

    Your car sits outside all day, you have a black interior or a big back glass, you drive for work, or you have kids in the back seat in July. The extra infrared rejection is noticeable on hot days.

  • Mixing is fine

    Some customers put Ceramic Plus on the front doors, where the 35% limit makes heat rejection matter most, and Ceramic IR in back. Ask and we’ll price it.

    Text (502) 631-0419 with your vehicle and how you use it, and Shane will tell you which one makes sense. No upsell needed.

Ceramic vs basic dyed vs carbon tint

Basic dyed film is the cheapest. Dye darkens the glass, nothing in it stops much infrared heat, and it fades toward purple over a few years in the sun.Carbon film uses carbon particles for color. It holds up better than basic dyed film and blocks more heat, but less than ceramic.Ceramic film adds ceramic technology that’s especially good at blocking infrared. HITEK builds Ceramic IR on carbon-based technology, and Ceramic Plus on a nano ceramic and IR coating. Questions we get a lot

Is ceramic tint worth it in Louisville?

For most drivers, yes. Summers here are hot and humid, the front doors can’t be darker than 35%, and ceramic is what blocks the heat without going darker.

What’s the difference between HITEK Ceramic IR and Ceramic Plus?

Both are ceramic. HITEK rates Ceramic IR at 65% total solar energy rejection and 75% infrared rejection, and Ceramic Plus at 69% and 90%. Ceramic Plus costs more and blocks more heat.

Does ceramic tint block phone signal?

No. Ceramic film has no metal layer, so phone, GPS, radio and key fob signals pass through normally.

Does a lighter ceramic film still block heat?

Yes. HITEK rates Ceramic Plus at its light 50% shade at 56% total solar energy rejection and 90% infrared rejection.

How long does ceramic tint last?

The life of the car, under normal use. Both films carry HITEK’s lifetime guarantee.
