Why HITEK

A tint job is only as good as the film and the person installing it. HITEK ceramic films go on clean and come with a lifetime guarantee that’s honored across the country, not just at one shop. That matters if you move, or if you’re stationed at Fort Knox and get orders.

We picked two HITEK films that block real heat even at lighter shades, which matters in Kentucky, where the front doors top out at 35%.

HITEK Ceramic IR

HITEK’s entry-level ceramic, built on carbon-based technology, and the film most of our customers choose. HITEK rates it at 65% total solar energy rejection and 75% infrared rejection (740–2500 nm, at the 5% shade), and it comes in more shades than any other HITEK line: 2, 5, 15, 20, 30, 35, 40, 45, 50 and 70%.

On a car, Ceramic IR starts at $249 for the side windows and back glass.

HITEK’s Ceramic IR page

HITEK Ceramic Plus

HITEK calls it true nano ceramic and IR coating technology. The version we install is rated 90% infrared rejection at every shade HITEK measures, with total solar energy rejection from 56% at the 50% shade to 69% at the darkest. HITEK backs it with a lifetime guarantee.

On a car, Ceramic Plus starts at $349.

HITEK’s Ceramic Plus page

About the lifetime guarantee

HITEK’s lifetime guarantee is honored nationwide, which means any authorized HITEK dealer can look at a warranty issue, not only the shop that installed it. Keep your paperwork; it lists the film, the shade and the install date.

The warranty covers the film. Damage from scratches, scraping the glass, or a window regulator that grinds the film is not a film defect. If something looks wrong in the first couple of weeks, bring it back and Shane will take a look.
