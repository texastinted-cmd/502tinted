How long does window tint take to cure in Louisville?

Three to five days most of the year, longer in winter. Here’s what’s normal, what isn’t, and how to take care of new film.

By Shane Crosier, owner of 502 Tinted · Updated October 21, 2026

Short answer: Window tint usually needs three to five days to cure in Louisville, and up to a week or more in cold, damp winter weather. In hot, dry summer weather it can be closer to two or three days. Keep the windows rolled up during that time. Haze and small water pockets while it cures are normal and go away on their own.

Why tint needs to cure

Film goes on with a slip solution so it can be positioned and squeegeed tight. A thin layer of that moisture stays behind the film and has to evaporate through it. Until it does, the adhesive hasn’t fully bonded, and rolling the window down can catch the edge and peel it.

Louisville weather, season by season

SeasonTypical cure timeWhy
Summer2–3 daysHeat and sun drive the moisture out fast
Spring and fall3–5 daysMild days, cool nights and rain slow it down
Winter5–10 daysCold and damp air keep moisture in the film longer

Parking in the sun helps. A garage in winter slows it down. Ceramic film cures the same way other films do.

What’s normal in the first week

  • A hazy or cloudy look, especially in the morning
  • Small water pockets or blisters that shrink day by day
  • A slight streaky look at certain angles while it dries

What isn’t normal

  • Bubbles that are still there after two weeks
  • Specks of dirt or hair trapped under the film
  • An edge that lifts or peels
  • A gap at the top edge of a side window that you weren’t told about

If you see any of those, text a photo to (502) 631-0419 and bring it back. That’s what the follow-up is for.

Taking care of new tint

  • Keep the windows up for three to five days, longer in winter
  • Don’t clean the inside of the glass for the first week
  • After that, use an ammonia-free glass cleaner and a soft microfiber towel
  • Be careful with seat belt buckles, rings and ice scrapers on the inside of the glass
  • The rear defroster is safe to use once the film has cured

Questions we get a lot

How long before I can roll down my windows after tint?

Three to five days in most Louisville weather, and up to a week or more in a cold, damp winter.

Is it normal for new tint to look hazy?

Yes. Haze and small water pockets are normal while the film cures and fade over several days.

Can I wash my car after getting tint?

Yes, the outside. Wait a week before cleaning the inside of the tinted glass, then use an ammonia-free cleaner.
