Book an interior detail

Short answer: An interior detail at 502 Tinted starts at $149 for a car. It includes a full vacuum with the trunk, shampooed carpets and mats, cleaned seats (leather cleaned and conditioned), the dash, console, door panels and vents, and the glass. Pet hair removal ($40), stain extraction ($40 per area) and odor treatment ($59) are add-ons.

What’s in an interior detail

  • Vacuum, everywhere. Under the seats, the seat tracks, between the console and the seats, and the trunk.
  • Carpets and mats. Shampooed and extracted, which is how winter salt stains come out.
  • Seats. Cloth is cleaned and extracted. Leather is cleaned with a leather-safe cleaner and conditioned so it doesn’t dry out.
  • Hard surfaces. Dash, console, cup holders, door panels and vents, cleaned and finished without a greasy shine.
  • Glass. Inside and out, including the top edge of the side windows that hides under the seal.

When an interior detail makes sense

After winter, when the carpets are crusted with salt. Before selling or trading in a car, when the interior is most of the first impression. After a road trip with kids or dogs. Or when you just want the car to feel new again.

If you’re tinting the windows too, book the interior first. Clean glass and a clean door panel mean a cleaner tint job.

Questions we get a lot

How much is an interior detail in Louisville?

At 502 Tinted, an interior detail starts at $149 for a car. Pet hair, heavy stains and large SUVs cost more.

How long does an interior detail take?

Two to four hours for most cars. Heavy pet hair or stains can add time.

Can you get salt stains out of carpet?

Usually, yes. Shampooing and hot-water extraction dissolves the white salt lines that build up over a Kentucky winter.

Can you remove smoke smell?

A full interior detail plus odor treatment ($59) takes care of most smells. Years of heavy smoking may need more than one round.

Do you clean leather seats?

Yes. Leather is cleaned with a pH-safe cleaner and conditioned.
