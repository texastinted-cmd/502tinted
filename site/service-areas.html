Louisville and Jefferson County

  • Valley Station

    Most Valley Station customers are a few minutes away on surface streets, no highway needed. Drop the car off on your way somewhere and pick it up the same afternoon.

  • Pleasure Ridge Park

    Head south on Dixie Highway into Valley Station. For most of PRP it’s a short drive, about ten minutes.

  • Shively

    Take Dixie Highway south through Pleasure Ridge Park into Valley Station, about 15 minutes.

  • Fairdale

    Head west across the south end of the county toward Valley Station, about 15 to 20 minutes depending on where you start.

  • Okolona

    Take the Gene Snyder west toward Dixie Highway and Valley Station, about 20 minutes.

  • St. Matthews

    Take I-264 (the Watterson) around to Dixie Highway, then south to Valley Station, about 30 minutes.

  • Jeffersontown

    Take the Gene Snyder Freeway west all the way to Dixie Highway, then into Valley Station, about 30 minutes.

Bullitt County

  • Hillview

    Take the Gene Snyder west toward Dixie Highway and Valley Station, about 20 to 25 minutes.

  • Shepherdsville

    Take I-65 north to the Gene Snyder, then west to Dixie Highway and Valley Station, about 25 minutes.

  • Mount Washington

    Take KY 44 west to Shepherdsville and I-65 north, or Bardstown Road to the Gene Snyder, then west to Valley Station. Either way, about 30 minutes.

Hardin County

  • Radcliff & Fort Knox

    Take US 31W north through Fort Knox and Muldraugh into Valley Station, about 30 minutes from Radcliff.

  • Elizabethtown

    Take US 31W north through Radcliff and Fort Knox into Valley Station, or I-65 north to the Gene Snyder. About 45 minutes either way.

Same law everywhere in Kentucky

Kentucky’s tint law is statewide, so it doesn’t matter which county you live in: 35% on the front doors, 18% behind the driver, 8% behind the driver on SUVs and vans labeled MPV, and specific rules for the windshield.

Every job is done at the shop, where we have the light, the clean bay and the tools to do it right. Most tint jobs and details are finished the same day, so even customers coming from Elizabethtown or Jeffersontown only make one trip.
